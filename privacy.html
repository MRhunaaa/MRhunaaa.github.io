Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

  1. Data we access. Only the basic profile information of the account owner's own TikTok account (user.info.basic: display name, avatar, open ID), to confirm which account is connected, and the permission to upload and publish videos (video.upload, video.publish).
  2. What we don't collect. We do not collect, read or store data about other TikTok users. We do not read followers, likes, comments or messages, and we do not use data for advertising.
  3. Storage. The access token is stored by our integration provider (Composio) only to post videos when the owner asks. Videos are stored on the owner's own computer.
  4. Sharing. We do not sell or share any data with third parties, except the TikTok API calls needed to publish a video.
  5. Removal. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or by disconnecting the account in Composio; the token then stops working.
  6. Contact. Through the contact details on the "Jangan Dijawab" YouTube channel.
