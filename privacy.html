Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

  1. Data we access. Only data of the account owner's own TikTok account: basic profile (user.info.basic: open ID, display name, avatar) and profile details (user.info.profile: username, profile link) to confirm which account is connected; account totals (user.info.stats: follower, following, like and video counts) shown on the owner's dashboard; the owner's own public video list (video.list) so an episode is not posted twice; and permission to upload and publish the owner's videos (video.upload, video.publish).
  2. What we don't collect. We do not collect, read or store data about other TikTok users. We do not read the identities of followers, or comments or messages, and we do not use data for advertising.
  3. Storage. The access token is stored by our integration provider (Composio) only to post videos when the owner asks. Videos are stored on the owner's own computer.
  4. Sharing. We do not sell or share any data with third parties, except the TikTok API calls needed to publish a video.
  5. Removal. The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or by disconnecting the account in Composio; the token then stops working.
  6. Contact. Through the contact details on the "Jangan Dijawab" YouTube channel.
